# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this workshop and repository to be a welcoming, harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of experience level or background. Learning
in public — asking "beginner" questions, making mistakes on branches, getting
things wrong the first time — is exactly what this space is for.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps everyone learn:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being patient with people who are new to the tools
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, personal attacks, and any form of
discrimination.

## Scope and enforcement

This Code of Conduct applies in all workshop spaces — the repository, chat, and
the room. If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the
facilitators. All reports will be handled discreetly.

This is a short, workshop-friendly summary. Many projects adopt the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/); reading a real
one is part of Session 1.
